<h2>GAP (Bridging the GAP between Geomatics, Architects, and Planners) – Level 2</h2>
<p></p>
<h4>About the Course</h4>

Following the success of GAP Level 1, the Level 2 training program delves deeper into `advanced geospatial applications` tailored for architects and planners. The course focuses on hands-on tools and techniques such as `LULC mapping, UAV-based analysis, BIM-GIS integration, and spatial machine learning` — equipping participants to address complex urban and environmental challenges with geospatial intelligence.
